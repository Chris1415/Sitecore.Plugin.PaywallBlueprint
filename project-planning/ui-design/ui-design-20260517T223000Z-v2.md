## 1. Design context & assumptions

- **Audience:**
  - **Primary** — OSS blueprint adopters scanning the bento to learn the freemium-display pattern; their first impression must read as "publication-grade dashboard", not "demo app". They will fork and reskin, so the hierarchy must be expressive enough to inspire variation, yet system-honest enough to keep their swap-in cards from looking out of place.
  - **Secondary** — Tenant editors using paywall-blueprint as a reference deployment inside Cloud Portal. They want their tenant name, sites count, and recent webhook traffic at a glance, and they want the premium tease to feel aspirational rather than spammy.

- **Goals and success criteria:**
  - G1 — Bento renders cleanly inside the `xmc:fullscreen` iframe (1024–1920px typical width); 11 cards visible in two reading rhythms (free, then premium) without horizontal scroll.
  - G2 — Premium gating is visually unambiguous and editorial — the Subscribe banner takes a hero slot in the masonry, not a floating popover.
  - G3 — Unlock feels like a magazine spread settling into place — heroes stagger in first, supporting cards follow.
  - G5 — Light and dark themes both feel finished; the asymmetric layout reads as intentional in either.

- **Constraints (brand, a11y, stack):**
  - Blok design system; Tailwind v4 / Nova preset (CSS-first tokens in `@theme inline { … }`); no `tailwind.config.ts`.
  - CSS animations only — no `framer-motion`, `motion`, `lottie`. Recharts built-ins allowed for P1.
  - WCAG AA contrast in both themes; visible focus rings via `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring`.
  - DOM order = visual reading order (FR-5.2) — binding constraint for the stagger-in cascade.
  - No hex literals in `site/components/bento/**` or `site/components/theme-toggle.tsx` (enforced by `test:no-hex-in-bento`).
  - No color-emoji codepoints (`❌` `✅` `⚠️`) — use lucide-react icons or monochrome glyphs.
  - Locked premium region is `aria-hidden="true"`; only the Subscribe banner is interactive in locked state.
  - All 6 premium cards are 100% fake data (ADR-0018) — no fetches at any state.
  - Theme toggle is **always visible** in topbar (ADR-0016).

- **Reference / inspiration:**
  - **Magazine front pages** (FT Weekend, Bloomberg Businessweek, Nieman Lab) — bold lead story, supporting columns, scanned in a Z-pattern.
  - **Linear's changelog pages** — hero card with smaller secondary tiles; restrained motion; deep slate palette in dark mode.
  - **Vercel dashboard** — asymmetric tile grid with hero charts and small KPI cards; sophisticated data-viz mood.
  - **Notion's gallery view** — masonry with size variation as information-priority signal.
  - All references serve as mood inputs, not pixel templates. The visual language remains Blok's.

- **Answers to clarifying questions:**
  - Q: Hero card count and placement?
    A: **Two heroes** — F1 Welcome (top-left, 2 cols × 1 row) and P1 Activity Chart (premium row, 2 cols × 1 row). The Subscribe banner takes hero shape in locked state.
  - Q: Should the Subscribe banner overlay the blurred region or live in the masonry?
    A: **Live in the masonry as a hero tile**, positioned where P1 + P5 would visually sit. This is v2's distinguishing move — the banner is part of the editorial rhythm, not a popover on top of it. (Premium cards still mount behind the banner, blurred, so the unlock animation has a smooth transition: banner unmounts → P1 + P5 reveal in those slots.)
  - Q: P4 Engagement Metrics 2-col-span vs single tile?
    A: **2-col-span banner row** with 4 inline KPI tiles. Mirrors editorial "by the numbers" callouts in magazines.
  - Q: Hero cards get larger type than support cards?
    A: **Yes** — heroes use `--font-size-4xl` for titles and KPIs; support cards stay at `--font-size-xl`/`2xl`. Adds intentional hierarchy without crowding the type ramp.
  - Q: Stagger interval — same 80ms across all cards, or longer for heroes?
    A: **100ms per step** for premium stagger-in (the spec's `80ms` from FR-5.2 is acceptable; v2 lengthens to 100ms to give the editorial pacing more breath — see § 9 for the rationale and the keyframe override.) **DOM order matches reading order**, which the masonry-via-grid-areas approach guarantees.

- **Assumptions made:**
  - Blok primitives `@blok/card`, `@blok/badge`, `@blok/button`, `@blok/skeleton`, `@blok/progress`, `@blok/separator`, `@blok/tooltip`, `@blok/dropdown` (dropdown-menu equivalent), `@blok/alert` are all available via `npx shadcn@latest add @blok/<name>`. Verified against the catalog in `sitecore:blok-components`. Quickstart only ships a subset (`alert`, `badge`, `button`, `card`, `collapsible`, `separator`, `skeleton`) — the rest install at Tranche A.
  - Blok semantic tokens used: `--background`, `--foreground`, `--muted`, `--muted-foreground`, `--card`, `--card-foreground`, `--border`, `--primary`, `--primary-foreground` (with `--background` fallback per the theming pitfall), `--ring`, `--accent`, `--accent-foreground`, `--destructive`. All available in the Nova preset's `@theme inline` block by default.
  - `@blok/progress` accepts a `value` prop and animates fill via CSS `transition: transform`. If the registry version doesn't animate by default, P2 uses CSS `transform: scaleX()` on a child of `@blok/progress` to control fill.
  - Recharts is theme-aware via `useTheme()` → token-derived `hsl(var(--primary))` strings passed as `stroke`/`fill` props. Verified in the architecture § 7e.
  - The Topbar component (`@blok/topbar`) is already installed from PRD-000; we extend its `rightSideItems[]` slot rather than re-installing.

## 2. Design direction

### Direction name: **Editorial Cadence**

> A magazine-spread dashboard. Two heroes anchor the page; supporting cards orbit them in column-faithful rhythm. The eye lands on the welcome headline, drops to the sites count and plan badge to its right, follows down to recent activity, then crosses the premium fold to the activity chart — the same scan a reader gives a Bloomberg Businessweek cover page. Where Variant 1 (fixed grid) treats every card as a peer, Editorial Cadence has a clear protagonist on each row.

### Visual mood

**Sophisticated data viz, in the editorial register.** Restrained motion (single keyframe per card, 100ms stagger, no springs). Deep card surfaces with subtle borders rather than heavy shadows — the kind of card you'd see in a financial pub's web edition. Type does the heavy lifting: hero titles are large and weight-confident; supporting card titles are quieter; KPI numerals are display-weight where they're the protagonist (P4), regular elsewhere. Color is reserved — Blok's `--primary` appears only on the Subscribe banner CTA, the plan badge, and premium "Premium" corner badges, so when it lands it actually means something. Charts use `--primary` as the dominant stroke against a `--muted` grid.

### Layout and structure

**4-column CSS Grid, masonry-by-areas.** Desktop ≥1024px:

```
Row 1 (free, height: 200px):     F1 F1 F2 F3
Row 2 (free, height: 200px):     F1 F1 F4 F4    ← F1 spans 2 rows (welcome hero is the tallest free card)
                                                  F4 takes 2 cols to give recent activity room
Row 3 (free spacer/F5, 140px):   F5 F5 F5 F5    ← extension points strip across full width
                                                  (chips list — wide & short)
Row 4 (premium, 260px):          P1 P1 P2 P3    ← P1 Activity Chart hero, 2 cols
Row 5 (premium, 200px):          P4 P4 P4 P4    ← KPI strip across full width (4 inline tiles)
Row 6 (premium, 200px):          P5 P5 P6 P6    ← AI insights + engagement score, paired
```

**DOM order = reading order:**
`F1 → F2 → F3 → F4 → F5 → P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 → P5 → P6` — exactly the order a Western reader scans (top-left first, sweep right, drop down, repeat). The CSS `grid-template-areas` notation in § 6 enforces this without auto-placement.

### Key components and patterns

- `@blok/card` — every card surface. Hero cards (F1, P1, P4 KPI strip, P5+P6 pair, F5 chip strip) use `@blok/card` with `--card` background + `border` of `border-border` and slightly stronger shadow (`shadow-sm` in light, `shadow-md` in dark) to signal protagonist status.
- `@blok/badge` — F1 plan badge, F5 extension-point chips, premium corner "Premium" badges. `colorScheme="primary"` for the Premium badges (verify `--primary-foreground` pair per theming pitfall; fall back to `text-background`).
- `@blok/button` — Subscribe CTA, Retry links, ThemeToggle trigger.
- `@blok/skeleton` — free-card loading; `@blok/skeleton` shapes inside `<PremiumPlaceholder>` for locked premium silhouettes.
- `@blok/progress` — P2 Content Health bars.
- `@blok/separator` — used sparingly inside multi-section cards (e.g. P5 between bullets) and inside F1 between greeting line and metadata.
- `@blok/tooltip` — premium corner badges show "Subscribe to unlock" on hover.
- `@blok/dropdown` (registry slug equivalent of `dropdown-menu`) — ThemeToggle 3-option menu.
- `@blok/alert` — free-card error state.
- `lucide-react` icons — `Sun`, `Moon`, `Monitor` (toggle), `Activity`, `Globe`, `Sparkles`, `TrendingUp`, `CheckCircle2`, `XCircle`, `Lock`, `Plug`, `User`. All inherit `currentColor`.

## 3. Key screens and flows

### Screens

The bento is one screen with two visible states driven by `useEntitlement`:

1. **`/full-page` — Locked state** (`tenant_no_subscription`)
   - Topbar (with always-visible ThemeToggle)
   - Free row (F1–F5) rendered with real data
   - Premium row rendered behind blur wrapper (`filter: blur(12px) opacity(0.7) pointer-events:none aria-hidden`)
   - Subscribe banner mounted into the masonry as a hero-shaped tile, positioned absolutely over the P1+P5 area (covers the chart hero + adjacent insights pair so the most informationally rich region is what the user is being asked to unlock)
   - Each blurred premium card shows a "Premium" badge in its corner (Z-index above blur)
   - Shimmer overlay loops across the locked region

2. **`/full-page` — Unlocked state** (`allowed`)
   - Same Topbar
   - Same free row
   - Premium row revealed; banner unmounted
   - 6 premium cards stagger-in over ~700ms (100ms × 6 + 100ms tail) in DOM order
   - P1 Recharts area animates on mount; P2 progress bars fill; P4 counters tick from 0; P6 progress ring animates

### Critical user flows

**Flow A — First-time tenant lands**
1. Iframe loads `/full-page`; `MarketplaceProvider` handshakes (~300ms)
2. Topbar paints with theme toggle in `rightSideItems[]`
3. Free cards mount; F2 starts `xmc.sites.listSites` query (skeleton until response)
4. Server-rendered F3 + F4 land with data from `SupabaseStore`
5. Premium region mounts in locked state — silhouettes appear, blur applies, Subscribe banner overlays the hero region
6. Shimmer animation starts looping; user reads the Subscribe banner copy and clicks
7. Existing `PaywallCheckoutDialog` (PRD-001) opens

**Flow B — Theme toggle**
1. User clicks `<Sun />` icon in topbar (assuming current = dark)
2. `@blok/dropdown` opens with Light / Dark / System options
3. User picks Light; `next-themes` `setTheme('light')` runs
4. `.dark` class removes from `<html>`; all Blok tokens flip
5. Recharts area chart re-renders with new theme-derived `stroke`/`fill`
6. Shimmer gradient (uses `hsl(var(--muted) / 0.12)`) re-renders with new muted color

**Flow C — Unlock**
1. User pays €0.99 via Stripe Checkout (PRD-001 unchanged)
2. `/paywall-return` posts back; `useEntitlement` reload fires
3. Page re-renders with `allowed`
4. Premium region wrapper loses `--locked` class; blur + opacity + shimmer + `aria-hidden` all clear
5. Subscribe banner unmounts (its DOM removed from the tree)
6. 6 premium cards apply `.bento-card--premium` class → `@keyframes fadeUp` runs with per-card `animation-delay` (0, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500ms)
7. Recharts `isAnimationActive` draws area path; counter animations tick

### Responsiveness

- **Desktop ≥1024px** — full 4-col masonry as specified
- **Tablet 768–1024px** — collapses to 2-col masonry; heroes become full-row spans
- **Mobile <768px** — single-column stack, full bleed; all 11 cards in DOM/reading order

Detail in § 5 & § 6.

### Accessibility notes

- All `@blok/card` surfaces use semantic structure: `<section>` with `<h3>` title where appropriate; KPI tiles inside P4 use `<div role="group" aria-labelledby="...">`.
- Premium region wrapper: `aria-hidden="true"` in locked state; restored to default (interactive) on unlock.
- Subscribe banner: focusable `<button>` for CTA; banner itself is `role="region"` with `aria-labelledby` pointing to its heading.
- ThemeToggle: `aria-label="Toggle theme"`; dropdown items have explicit text labels.
- Focus rings: every interactive element uses `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-ring focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-background`.
- Color contrast WCAG AA in both themes — verified at Tranche D manual smoke (NFR-2, NFR-6).
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` skips all stagger-in, shimmer, chart-draw, progress-fill, counter, and ring animations; final states render directly.

## 5. Responsiveness and accessibility

### Breakpoints

#### Desktop ≥1024px (4-col masonry)

Specified above.

#### Tablet 768–1024px (2-col masonry, heroes full-row)

```
Row 1 (220px):     F1 F1        ← Welcome remains hero, now full-row
Row 2 (180px):     F2 F3
Row 3 (180px):     F4 F4        ← Recent activity full-row
Row 4 (120px):     F5 F5        ← Extension points strip
Row 5 (260px):     P1 P1        ← Activity chart hero full-row
Row 6 (180px):     P2 P3
Row 7 (200px):     P4 P4        ← KPI strip full-row (4 inline tiles become 2x2)
Row 8 (180px):     P5 P6
```

### Unlocked-state spec (stagger-in cascade + animations)

#### Stagger-in delays — DOM order = reading order

v2 uses **100ms steps** (vs FR-5.2's nominal 80ms) to give the editorial pacing a touch more breath. The PRD's FR-5.2 lists the values `0ms, 80ms, 160ms, 240ms, 320ms, 400ms` as the baseline; v2's override:

```css
@keyframes fadeUp {
  from { opacity: 0; transform: translateY(16px); }
  to   { opacity: 1; transform: translateY(0); }
}

.bento-card--premium {
  animation: fadeUp 600ms ease-out forwards;
  opacity: 0; /* initial state — animation reveals */
}

/* v2 override: 100ms steps for editorial pacing */
.bento-card--premium:nth-child(1) { animation-delay: 0ms; }
.bento-card--premium:nth-child(2) { animation-delay: 100ms; }
.bento-card--premium:nth-child(3) { animation-delay: 200ms; }
.bento-card--premium:nth-child(4) { animation-delay: 300ms; }
.bento-card--premium:nth-child(5) { animation-delay: 400ms; }
.bento-card--premium:nth-child(6) { animation-delay: 500ms; }

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  .bento-card--premium {
    animation: none;
    opacity: 1;
    transform: none;
  }
}
```

**Note for QA:** v2's 100ms override means the AC3.5 acceptance criterion ("80ms per-card delay") changes to "100ms per-card delay" if v2 is selected. The PRD's wording "design-target 1.5s; manual smoke check at Tranche D" (AC3.8) still holds — total stagger duration on v2 = 500ms + 600ms card animation = 1.1s, well within budget.

**Total stagger budget:** 500ms (last card's delay) + 600ms (its fade duration) = **1.1s** from first frame to all-revealed; within the PRD's 1.5s design target.

#### Per-card unlock animations

- **P1 Activity Chart** — Recharts `<AreaChart isAnimationActive={true} animationDuration={800}>` draws the area path on mount. Path stroke `hsl(var(--primary))`; fill `hsl(var(--primary) / 0.18)`.
- **P2 Content Health** — Each `@blok/progress` bar fills from 0 to target via CSS `transform: scaleX(0 → value/100)` over 800ms ease-out, with 200ms staggered delays between bars (so the 3 bars feel intentional, not simultaneous).
- **P4 Engagement Metrics** — Counter animations on each KPI via `requestAnimationFrame` from 0 to final over 600ms ease-out (FR-5.5). Format-aware: integers count up to integer values; "4m 32s" counts seconds and formats; percent counts percentage.
- **P6 Engagement Score** — Progress ring animates `stroke-dashoffset` from full circumference to target over 800ms ease-out. Forecast sparkline animates via SVG `stroke-dasharray` "draw" pattern over 1000ms.

All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and skip to final state.

### Theme spec — light + dark explicit

The Nova preset ships dual-theme tokens out of the box. v2 uses them as-is; no new tokens introduced.

#### Light theme (`<html>` without `.dark`)

- `--background` = white-ish; `--foreground` = near-black slate
- Card surface `--card` = `--background` or one-step-elevated white
- Card border `--border` = subtle gray
- Card shadow on heroes (F1, P1, P4): `shadow-sm` (Blok small shadow) — additive emphasis
- Subscribe banner: `bg-card` + `ring-2 ring-primary` + `shadow-lg` keep the glow soft (per `sitecore:blok-marketplace-integration` iframe shadow guidance — large shadows clip)
- Recharts chart: stroke `hsl(var(--primary))` (Blok red/orange-ish brand primary), fill same with 18% alpha, grid `hsl(var(--border))`, ticks `hsl(var(--muted-foreground))`
- Locked region: blur visible; shimmer uses `hsl(var(--muted) / 0.12)` — pale, near-invisible but enough to suggest movement
- Premium "Premium" badges: `bg-primary text-primary-foreground` (or `text-background` fallback)

#### Dark theme (`<html class="dark">`)

- `--background` = deep slate (near-black)
- Card surface `--card` = one-step-elevated slate (slightly lighter)
- Heroes get **slightly more emphasis** in dark: `shadow-md` instead of `shadow-sm` (deeper for editorial drama)
- Subscribe banner: same structure; the primary-ring + tinted shadow read more dramatically against deep background — verify legibility of the CTA label, may need `text-background` fallback per QuickCopy v0.1 lesson
- Recharts chart: `useTheme()` reads `theme === 'dark'`; primary stroke remains `hsl(var(--primary))` (auto-flips); grid `hsl(var(--border))` (auto-flips)
- Locked region: blur reads slightly heavier on dark surfaces (lower-contrast silhouettes); shimmer color identical (`hsl(var(--muted) / 0.12)`) but appears slightly brighter relative to background — adjust to `0.08` if testing shows it's too prominent
- Premium "Premium" badges: same; verify contrast at Tranche D
